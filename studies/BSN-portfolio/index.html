🎯 Identifying the Gap

At Brown, students passionate about the sports industry lacked structured pathways into professional careers. I co-led the creation of Brown Sports Network (BSN) to build that bridge — connecting students with senior executives, securing real-world consulting projects, and launching mentorship pipelines.

In just over a year, BSN grew into one of Brown's fastest-expanding pre-professional networks, creating opportunities across sports business, media, tech, and operations.

Key Focus: Building bridges between Brown students and the sports industry through structured pathways, mentorship, and real-world projects.

👥 My Role: VP of Alumni & Analytics

  • Built and scaled a 40+ member alumni advisory board across NFL, NBA, EPL, MLB, and VC
  • Directed partnerships with teams and agencies (TSG Hoffenheim, JL Sports, Rhode Island FC)
  • Designed and launched the BSN Analytics consulting arm
  • Developed the BSN Speaker Series featuring C-level executives
  • Structured the mentorship program pairing students with sports industry leaders
  • Supported sponsor partnerships (Culture Pop Soda, Throne Sport Coffee, Lemon Perfect)
