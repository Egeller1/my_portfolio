🎯 Vision

I founded 12M Analytics to revolutionize decision-making for college football programs that lacked the resources of top-tier schools.

12M Analytics bridges the analytics gap at the FCS and DIII levels, empowering programs to unlock new levels of strategic advantage through real-time, actionable insights.

Key Focus: Delivering coach-optimized materials for in-game decision-making

🔍 Challenge

Despite football's increasing complexity, lower-division programs often operate without real-time strategic analytics — leading to predictable play-calling, missed in-game opportunities, and slower player development.

  • Predictable play-calling
  • Under-optimized in-game decisions
  • Inefficient player development

🎨 Building the Playbook for Smarter Football

Our services are designed for real-world, sideline decision-making — not just academic analysis.

Core Services

  • Self‐Scouting & Internal Analysis
  • League‐Wide Trend Benchmarking
  • Efficiency Maximization
  • Opponent Scouting & Game Preparation
  • Weekly Sideline‐Ready Reports

Weekly Analytics Package

Our centerpiece: a concise, double‐sided one‐pager delivering:

  • Fourth‐down and two‐point conversion recommendations
  • Expected points models for long‐yardage decisions
  • Opponent third-down breakdowns by distance and play tendency
  • League-wide benchmarking against division standards

Our Expected Points model empowers smarter 4th down decision-making in high-pressure moments.

📊 Case Study: Mass Maritime Academy

In Fall 2024, Mass Maritime partnered with 12M Analytics to overhaul their in-game strategy. Using 12M's customized weekly sheets, they improved their fourth down decision-making by 17%, gained an average of 83 more rushing yards per game, and posted their best offensive efficiency metrics in years.

👥 From Solo Founder to Leadership Team

I launched 12M Analytics as a solo founder, building and delivering all materials myself in the inaugural season. As traction grew, I expanded 12M by building a core leadership team including a Director of Analytics, a Director of Design, and a Director of Communications, laying the foundation for future analyst growth.
