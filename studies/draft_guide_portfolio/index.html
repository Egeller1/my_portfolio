🎯 Overview

Over two NFL Draft cycles, I independently produced two complete prospect guides, covering over 250 players and clocking over 400 hours of work across film study, metric analysis, writing, editing, and layout. Each guide blended traditional scouting methods with advanced data metrics like RAS (Relative Athletic Score), creating one of the largest independent draft resources published online.

While major draft publications rely on full teams to create similar guides, I handled the entire process end-to-end. From watching hundreds of hours of game film to logging athletic testing scores and creating player comparisons, I operated on a professional scouting calendar and delivered polished, scalable final products.

🎨 Process

Film Study

  • Watched multiple full games per prospect
  • Evaluated movement skills, decision-making, positional mastery

Metrics and Data Analysis

  • Integrated combine/pro day measurements and Relative Athletic Scores (RAS)
  • Quantified athletic ceilings alongside film analysis

Grading System

  • Developed a structured rubric covering size, athleticism, instincts, role projection, ceiling/floor

Profile Writing

  • Wrote full player breakdowns including strengths, weaknesses, and NFL role projections
  • Created player comparisons for quarterbacks and certain elite prospects

Editing and Layout

  • Organized players by position and overall rankings
  • Designed clickable tables of contents for easy navigation

💭 Reflection

"This project mirrors the work NFL teams do across a full scouting year—compressed into a solo operation."

Producing these NFL Draft Guides tested and sharpened every core skill I value: research, analysis, pattern recognition, high-volume writing, and deadline-driven project management. It challenged me to operate at the intersection of deep film study and metric-backed projection, and to deliver professional-grade scouting products at scale.

Through this project, I demonstrated the ability to self-direct large, complex initiatives, manage high-volume deliverables under tight timelines, and blend creative analysis with data-driven evaluation—a mindset I now apply to all my sports, analytics, and product work.
